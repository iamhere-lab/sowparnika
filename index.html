Sowparnika has been building homes in Bengaluru since 2003, with projects across Karnataka, Kerala and Tamil Nadu and a strong presence in East Bengaluru. Architecture, engineering, construction, interiors, plumbing and security are handled in-house, so one team answers for the whole job.

Roots for Tomorrow is the codename for Sowparnika The Native, the developer's 86th project: a community planned for families who want a well-built home near Whitefield without Whitefield prices. The project is registered with Karnataka RERA under PRM/KA/RERA/1250/304/PR/011026/008990.
